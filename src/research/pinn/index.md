## PINNs for Nonlinear Schrödinger

##### with V. Bokil,  R. Ceja, M. Fabien, R. Maity, V. Obieke, C. Scheid 

In 2024 during an ICERM workshop, we co-mentored with V. Bokil and C. Scheid a research group on structure preserving schemes for nonlinear optics. Starting with nonlinear Schrödinger equation, we investigated classical finite differences schemes that preserve mass and Hamiltonian, and explored physics-informed neural networks techniques taking into account mass and Hamiltonian preservation. 

A report is under preparation.
